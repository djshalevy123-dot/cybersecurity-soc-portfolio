AI-Assisted SOC Investigation - Splunk Queries
================================================

1. Search for the lab activity
--------------------------------
index=* sourcetype="XmlWinEventLog:Microsoft-Windows-Sysmon/Operational"
("AI-SOC-Test" OR "whoami.exe")


2. Build process timeline
--------------------------------
index=* sourcetype="XmlWinEventLog:Microsoft-Windows-Sysmon/Operational"
("AI-SOC-Test" OR "whoami.exe")
| rex field=_raw "<EventID>(?<EventID>\d+)</EventID>"
| rex field=_raw "Data Name='ProcessId'>(?<ProcessId>\d+)<"
| rex field=_raw "Data Name='Image'>(?<Image>[^<]+)<"
| rex field=_raw "Data Name='CommandLine'>(?<CommandLine>[^<]+)<"
| rex field=_raw "Data Name='User'>(?<User>[^<]+)<"
| rex field=_raw "Data Name='ParentProcessId'>(?<ParentProcessId>\d+)<"
| rex field=_raw "Data Name='ParentImage'>(?<ParentImage>[^<]+)<"
| table _time EventID ProcessId Image User ParentProcessId ParentImage CommandLine
| sort _time

6. Validate PID 6380 process activity by ProcessGuid
--------------------------------
index=* sourcetype="XmlWinEventLog:Microsoft-Windows-Sysmon/Operational"
| rex field=_raw "<EventID>(?<EventID>\d+)</EventID>"
| rex field=_raw "Data Name='ProcessGuid'>(?<ThisProcessGuid>[^<]+)<"
| rex field=_raw "Data Name='ProcessId'>(?<ProcessId>\d+)<"
| rex field=_raw "Data Name='Image'>(?<Image>[^<]+)<"
| rex field=_raw "Data Name='CommandLine'>(?<CommandLine>[^<]+)<"
| rex field=_raw "Data Name='TargetFilename'>(?<TargetFilename>[^<]+)<"
| search ThisProcessGuid="{abe1dc79-4432-6ab9-2801-000000002c00}"
| table _time EventID ProcessId Image CommandLine TargetFilename ThisProcessGuid
| sort _time
